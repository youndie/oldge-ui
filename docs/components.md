# Component catalogue

Which composable is the design system's which component, where it lives, and what shows it. Read
it at the start of a session instead of grepping for a component's file.

**The tables below are generated** by [`scripts/component_catalog.py`](../scripts/component_catalog.py)
from four things that cannot lie about them:

- the design system's own list of components, `reference/design-system/components/index.d.ts`;
- the public composables in oldge-core's sources;
- the parity references and goldens;
- the pages' screens in `sample`.

Editing the tables by hand is undone on the next run, and `make check` fails while they are
stale. The prose around them is hand-written.

## How to read a row

- **Composables**: the public composables that are this component. Most are `Oldge<Name>`. Chip is
  three, as Material's chips are; ListSection is a `LazyListScope` extension and its header; and a
  few have a second public form (the icon button, the extended Fab, the lazy pull to refresh). The
  map of those exceptions is the one hand-kept part of the script, and every name in it is checked
  against the sources.
- **File** and **What it is**: the first composable's file, and the first sentence of its KDoc. The
  rest of the KDoc names the README rules it implements.
- **References**: the design system's renders the component is held to by parity.
- **Goldens**: the fixture groups that render it, the preview's and the `States` it does not show.
  The goldens are the running instances: viddik's registry and `ScreenshotSuiteTest` hold each one
  to a fixture.
- **Beyond the exports**: what the library offers that the design system has as a class or a rule
  rather than a component (`og-body`, `og-actions`), or as the theme around everything.
